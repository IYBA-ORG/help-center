# Frequently Asked Questions — Help Center

## Which product should I choose?

Choose [IYBA](../iyba/index.md) for association and membership topics, [YachtBroker](../yachtbroker/index.md) for MLS and vessel listing topics, or [BoatDox](../boatdox/index.md) for deals and document topics.

## Do I need to sign in to read the help center?

No. This help center is publicly accessible. Your product may require a separate sign-in to use its features.

## How do I find a topic?

Use the search in the site header, or choose a product and browse its topics. Include the product name in your search to distinguish similar topics.

## Why does a page say “Guide in preparation”?

The topic has a place in the documentation, but its instructions have not yet been verified and published. A planned topic does not confirm that a feature is available for your account.

## Where can I get help while a guide is being prepared?

See [Contact Support](contact.md) for links to the official product websites.
